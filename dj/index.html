What kind of music do you play?

The signature direction is deep house and sophisticated lounge grooves. We’ll discuss your preferences and your crowd to see whether the sound is a good fit for your event.

Based in St. Louis, with a focus on local private events and upscale gatherings. Share your venue or location when you reach out so we can discuss logistics.

Quotes depend on your date, duration, venue, and event needs. Contact Jeff with the basics for availability and a tailored conversation. An inquiry does not reserve your date.
